I've attached the "standard" conference config. You can use Chirp, which is part of Kali, to program the radios. The standard conference config uses FRS/GMRS frequencies. FRS frequencies do not require a licensed individual and can be transmitted on freely. Technically, GMRS requires an FCC license, although no exam is required. Each individual must obtain a license, as entity licenses are no longer available.
